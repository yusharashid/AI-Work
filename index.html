01 — INTRODUCTION

What Is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables computers to learn, solve problems, and perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

02 — IMPORTANCE

Why Is AI Important?

  • It saves time.
  • It helps solve problems.
  • It supports better decisions.
  • It makes many tasks easier.

03 — CATEGORIES

Types of AI

  • Super AI — a hypothetical advanced AI.
